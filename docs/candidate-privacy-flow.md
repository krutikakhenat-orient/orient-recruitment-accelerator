# Candidate Privacy Flow

How a candidate's data is collected, used, and controlled.

## What is collected
Only what the campaign's approved form requests: contact details, experience, skills,
education, availability, a resume, and declarations. Compensation is collected only if the
campaign explicitly enables it. The form never collects date of birth, age, gender,
marital status, medical/disability information, religion, caste, race/ethnicity, sexual
orientation, photographs, vaccination status, hobbies, or family information — and those
are rejected if sent.

## Consent
- **Application consent** is mandatory to submit — the application is refused without it.
- **Talent-pool consent** is a separate, optional opt-in. It is never implied by applying.

## How the data is used
- The resume is scanned for malware and quarantined before any processing.
- Fields are extracted with a confidence score and the text span they came from; the
  candidate/HR can confirm or correct them. Corrections keep the original extracted value.
- Screening is deterministic and explainable. AI (if ever enabled) is advisory only and
  never makes or triggers the hiring decision.
- Internal scores and reviewer notes are never shown to the candidate or included in
  communications.

## Candidate rights
A candidate may request: access/export of their data, correction, deletion, restriction of
processing, and withdrawal of talent-pool consent. Each request is tracked with an
auditable lifecycle. Talent-pool consent can be withdrawn at any time, which removes the
candidate from pool searches.

## Retention
Data is kept per the campaign's retention policy for each record category. On expiry it is
notified/restricted/deleted/anonymised (or extended under an approved legal hold), and
every such action is logged.

## Note
This describes the platform's technical controls. It is not a legal notice and does not
claim compliance with any specific regulation on its own.
