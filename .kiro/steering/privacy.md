# Privacy

## Principles
- Data minimisation: collect only what a campaign needs for its stated purpose.
- Purpose-specific consent with versioned privacy notices.
- Candidate rights: correction, consent withdrawal, access/export, processing restriction,
  deletion, anonymisation.
- Talent-pool inclusion requires a **separate, explicit** opt-in consent, independent of
  the application consent.

## Excluded from automated screening (never used as criteria)
Date of birth, age, gender, marital status, medical condition, disability information,
religion, caste, race/ethnicity, sexual orientation, photograph, vaccination status,
hobbies, family information. Accessibility-assistance requests may be collected to provide
assistance but must never be used as a screening criterion.

## Retention
Configurable retention policies per record category: candidate identity, campaign
application, resume, screening result, interview assessment, candidate communication,
talent-pool record, audit evidence, security logs. Actions: notify owner, restrict access,
delete, anonymise, extend under approved legal hold. Every retention action is logged.
Destructive actions run only after the owner-notification window.

## Consent & requests
- Store consent version, date, expiry, permitted purposes, withdrawal date, retention status.
- Privacy requests have an auditable workflow and acknowledgement notifications.

## Compliance posture
Do not present the application as automatically legally compliant. Provide technical
controls that support organisational compliance.
